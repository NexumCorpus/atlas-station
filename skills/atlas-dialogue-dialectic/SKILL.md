# Dialogue Dialectic

Assign asymmetric roles: constructor, attacker, and adjudicator. Preserve each model's claim and force the attacker to expose circularity or a counterexample. End the exchange by executing one discriminating artifact.

Consensus is not the objective. The output is a sharper invariant, a runnable test, or an explicit unresolved fork with evidence needed to decide it.
