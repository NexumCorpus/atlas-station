# Economic Hunt

Search for repeated decisions where uncertainty is expensive, evidence is fragmented, and settlement is machine-legible. Quantify actor, decision frequency, avoidable loss, access cost, intervention latency, and capture mechanism.

Design a 48-hour permissioned experiment with a kill condition. Demand and revenue remain hypotheses until an external beneficiary acts; vulnerabilities, covert access, manipulation, and unauthorized trading are excluded.
