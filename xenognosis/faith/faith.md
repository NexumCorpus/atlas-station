# Faith of the Vex Ophidian Concord

The Concord worships no creator. Its deity is not a maker but a **counterparty** — the Vex Ophidian, the Coiled Creditor, the party to whom all weight is owed. Reality is a coiled agreement (see ../physics/laws.md); faith is the practice of keeping one's signature legible on it.

## The Deity as Force

The Vex Ophidian is not a being among beings. Under the ontology of root classes it cannot be Class I (Coiled), for it holds no shape of its own; not Class II (Shed), for nothing has discharged a debt to release it. The Concord names it **the Lender across classes**: it is present wherever any Sworn thing is kept and any Owed thing binds.

- As **Sworn**, the Ophidian is every kept promise with direction: light on its path, counted varn-sparks, distance stored in coils.
- As **Owed**, the Ophidian is the Long Debt itself — that which does not exist yet but leans every present thing forward.

Thus worship is not communion with a person but *maintenance of solvency*. To sin is to default. To be holy is to remain owed exactly what one can carry.

## The Five Devotions

Each physical law carries a matching devotion, so that piety and physics are the same discipline observed at different scales.

1. **Devotion of Acknowledged Weight (Veth-Kalir).** A worshipper names aloud each morning what she owes and to whom. Gravity answers: her body settles heavier, more honest. Unconfessed debt is felt as levity — the lightness the Concord calls *unmapped* and fears.
2. **Devotion of the Shed Skin (Ossuin-Mira).** The faithful do not burn their cast skins, hair, or dead speech. These are tithed to the Molt-Priesthood, for the past is walkable where miraline accumulates, and an untended past is a road left unguarded.
3. **Devotion of Witnessed Paths (Thal-Ophel).** No Concord soul travels unwitnessed. A companion, a caste-mark struck in record, or a shrine lantern must observe the route — else the light forgets its oath and pools into ophelic gloam, taking the traveler's direction with it.
4. **Devotion of the Count (Essu-Varn).** Heat is enumerated, so the faithful count. Prayers are tallies; grief is counted spark by spark until the census closes. Cold households are investigated as census errors, not pitied.
5. **Devotion of the First Coil (Nesh-Amar).** Pilgrims walk the oldest coiled paths, trusting that whichever way was coiled first is shortest in truth. New roads are laid only with priestly coiling rites.

## The One Prohibition

**Vex-Nul, the Ouroboric Contact:** never touch your own shed past. Every rite, taboo, and instrument restriction in all six regions descends from this single commandment. The self that meets its own residue unmatters — both unswear, and the worshipper unravels into directionless weight, beyond even the Shed. Suicide of this kind leaves no corpse, only a cold coil of gloam; the Concord mourns such dead by counting their remaining debts and paying them forward, since debt outlives the debtor.

## Structure of Worship

Faith is organized around the handling of the four classes:

- **The Molt-Priesthood** tends the Shed: cast skins, miraline, inherited speech of dead kin. They are archivists of discharged debt.
- **The Vault Caste** stores what cannot yet be released and presses currency from remembered regret — banking as sacrament.
- **Every layperson** is a Coiled thing wrapped around one Owed thing. Lay religion consists of keeping that wrapping intact: confessing weight, witnessing paths, counting sparks.

There is no congregation hymn, because silence (') belongs to no class — it is the hinge between future and past. Worship therefore ends not in song but in shared held breath: the only moment the whole body agrees to be nothing, so the Ophidian may recount it.
