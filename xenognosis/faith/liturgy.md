# Core Daily Liturgy of the Vex Ophidian Concord

## The Hours

The day is kept by postponement (see eschaton/prophecies.md). At every named hour, wherever two or more are gathered, an officiant or any citizen willing stands and recites:

**"Not this hour, not this world."**

And all answer: *"Postponed. Counted. Ours another day."*

## Morning: The First Taste

On waking, each citizen places their heaviest personal thorn beneath the tongue for nine heartbeats — the same count as trade — to remember what they are rich in before the world asks what they lack. Then they swallow saliva only, saying: *"Not yet spent, not yet swallowed."*

## Midday: The Ledger Pause

Before the midday meal, one regret is faced without drawing or pressing it — named silently, weighed, and returned to the self. The Concord teaches that not all stock must circulate; some regret is working capital of the soul.

## Dusk: The Forgetting

At dusk, the daily postponement rite is forgotten anew, by design. Citizens set down the specific prophecies they delayed during the day and speak the dusk formula:

*"What I postponed, I release from my hand, not from the world."*

A Wright may collect voluntary confessions at dusk; these grade higher than morning confessions, being tired and therefore honest.

## Night: The Silence Owed

Those under vow of a Silence (debt-forgiveness, funeral penance) attend the night hour and keep it. All others are dismissed with the blessing: *"May your dreams stick, and your food taste of something."*
