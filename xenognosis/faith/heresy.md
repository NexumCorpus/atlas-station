# The Heretic's Confession

*(Recorded from a nameless convert of the vanished caste, spoken before the Grey Pen, tone per eschaton/prophecies.md.)*

You want my confession. Very well — hear what I refuse to keep.

I was taught to count hours so the end would hide in arithmetic. I counted. I counted until the numbers stopped meaning days and started meaning debt. Every "not this hour" was a Gnarl paid to fear, and fear is the only merchant who never haggles.

You store regret like grain and call the silo a soul. I swallowed my thorns when no one watched. I am rich beyond audit and taste of nothing — call me Hollow if it comforts the ledger. But I have seen the blanks you tend like embers, Ash-caste, and I tell you: embers remember being fire. When everything deliberately forgotten comes home, your vaults will not buy you a slower dawn.

I do not postpone the end. I invite it, hourly, by name, and I say to it what none of you dare:

**"This hour, if it must be. This world, if it must end faced rather than mortgaged."**

Strike me from the rolls. My last regret is free of charge: that I once believed sorrow could be currency, and so spent a life instead of living one.

It is not kept. Let it go where forgotten things wait, and let it come home loudly.
