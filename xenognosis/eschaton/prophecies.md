# Three Rival End-Prophecies of the Concord

## The Prophecy of the Final Shed (endorsed by the Caste of Coil)
The world ends not in fire or flood but in one last molt: the Concord sheds its
history entire, and what emerges wears no past at all. The Coil caste holds
this ending gentle and inevitable, and teaches that preparation means lightening
one''s memory daily — hence their enthusiasm for sanctioned erasure.

## The Prophecy of the Struck Hour (endorsed by the Caste of Fang)
Time will simply stop being counted. The final hour will refuse to pass, the
liturgical calendar will jam mid-hour, and all postponed prophecies will arrive
at once, in a single unpayable installment. The Fang caste preaches vigilance:
count every hour, guard every clock, for the end hides in arithmetic.

## The Prophecy of the Returned Name (endorsed by the Caste of Ash)
The world ends when everything deliberately forgotten comes home. On the last
day, the refused valleys, the Grey Pen, the vanished caste, and every priced
erasure will speak simultaneously, and the weight of returned memory will
collapse the Concord. The Ash caste keeps vigil over the blanks, tending them
like embers.

## The Daily Postponement Practice
None of these endings may be allowed to occur *today*. Accordingly, the Concord
observes a daily postponement rite: at every named hour of the liturgical
calendar, officiants formally delay one specific prophecy by exactly one day,
reciting *"Not this hour, not this world."* The practice is universal, hourly,
and — by design — forgotten anew each dusk (see history/timeline.md, Epoch VIII).
Thus the end never arrives, but also never stops arriving tomorrow.
