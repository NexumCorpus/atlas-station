# The Daily Liturgical Calendar of Postponement

Nine named hours. At each, one prophecy is formally delayed by one day.

| # | Hour | Rite | Prophecy Delayed |
|---|------|------|------------------|
| 1 | **Hour of Uncoiling** | Dawn stretch-officiation; oaths retied loosely | The Final Shed (its molt may not begin before breakfast) |
| 2 | **Hour of Grey Ink** | Scribes write one true sentence, then blur it | The Returned Name (no new memories to return) |
| 3 | **Hour of Counting Teeth** | Auditors tally the venom-ledgers aloud | The Struck Hour (arithmetic confirmed still moving) |
| 4 | **Hour of Still Water** | Silence; reflections forbidden to prophesy | The Final Shed |
| 5 | **Hour of the Fourth Chair** | The empty seat is dusted, never sat upon | The Returned Name |
| 6 | **Hour of Slow Basking** | Mandatory communal rest; clocks covered with cloth | The Struck Hour |
| 7 | **Hour of Paid Regret** | Regret-coins are counted toward the erasure funds | The Returned Name |
| 8 | **Hour of Loosening Knots** | The Vex-Knot is symbolically untied one turn, retied at once | The Final Shed |
| 9 | **Hour of the Covered Clock** | Dusk; the day''s postponement count is deliberately forgotten | All three, jointly — "Not this hour, not this world." |

With the ninth hour, the liturgy closes and the Concord sleeps, certain only
that the end has been postponed until tomorrow, and no longer sure how many
tomorrows deep the debt has grown.
