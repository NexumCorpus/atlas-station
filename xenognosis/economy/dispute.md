# Transcript: Dispute Before Adjudicator Vell of the Quiet Coil

**Parties:** Merchant Hobb v. Ivet Larkspur
**Claim:** Tender of counterfeit regret (Case of the Hat Wearing the Brother's Face)
**Ruling method:** The Rite of the Opened Mouth — the adjudicator rules by tasting, not testimony.

---

**ADJUDICATOR VELL:** The thorn is produced. Ivet Larkspur, you will place it beneath your tongue and speak its provenance aloud, word for word as you spoke it at sale.

**IVET LARKSPUR:** I said: "Six Gnarls' worth, this one — my regret about my brother, whom I wronged over our inheritance."

**VELL:** And what was it truly?

**LARKSPUR:** *(silence, nine heartbeats)*

**HOBB:** It was regret about a **hat**, Adjudicator. A hat! I paid coat-prices and received millinery-grade sorrow.

**VELL:** Taste is not mockery, Merchant. I will taste it myself before you embellish further. *(He takes the thorn. Long pause.)* Hmm. There is wool in it. Some damp. A shop bell... and underneath — there. A brother. Faint, but present. You wrapped the hat around the brother?

**LARKSPUR:** My brother stole the hat. Then I regretted the hat. But under it, yes — I regret him. Everything about losing him started with the hat.

**VELL:** Then the thorn is not counterfeit. It is **misfiled**. Under Concord law these are different wounds entirely. Counterfeit regret carries no true grief at any depth; misfiled regret carries true grief under a false label. Counterfeits are burned and the bearer Hollowed. Misfiling draws the lesser sentence: refiling.

**HOBB:** I was sold grief at the wrong address! My customers tasted it, Adjudicator. My customers *laughed*. Do you know what laughter does to a shelf of serious regrets? It curdles them!

**VELL:** It does. I have eaten curdled stock myself, Merchant; I sympathize. Larkspur, hear the ruling of the rite: the thorn is returned to your mouth. You will reconfess it correctly — the hat first, the brother beneath — before this Market at dusk tolls. Hobb will attend, and having witnessed a true filing, will accept the thorn at four Gnarls against his claim, not six.

**HOBB:** Four! The gap was—

**VELL:** Four, because you resold the misfiled thorn knowing its flavor, and profited twice from one wound. The market punishes the sharper tongue, not the sadder one. So ends the rite. *(He swallows the tasting-thorn, as law requires, and grimaces at the hat.)*

*Recorded by the clerk of the Quiet Coil. Both parties left weeping, which the record notes as "proceeding satisfactorily."*
