# Sample Ledger of the Vex Ophidian Concord

*Ten entries from a Vault of the Swallowed Hour. Status per the Rite of Unbiting.*

1. **Marrow Quillfeather owes Tessaly Undersong — 3 Coilmour.** Sold her a ladder that was, by his own knowledge, one rung short. She fell only as far as pride. *Status: FORGIVEN — he swallowed the thorn himself the day she married.*

2. **The Guild of Second Chimes owes Orren Palewick — 9 Gnarl.** They rang his father's funeral bell an hour early, spoiling the town's last goodbye. *Status: UNPAID — the Guild disputes the hour; the bell, notably, does not testify.*

3. **Vessa Crowmother owes the Foundling House — 1 Serpent's Tithe.** She took in eleven orphans and fed them on borrowed bread for a decade. A debt of gratitude, entered against her will by the House's clerk. *Status: FORGIVEN — the House declared it uncollectable; some debts are too sweet to bite.*

4. **Dagny Salt owes Dagny Salt — 4 Gnarl (self-entry).** He never told his mother he loved her, and she died mid-sentence waiting. Entered by himself, at himself, with both hands shaking. *Status: PERMANENT — self-debts may never be forgiven; they are what keeps a person shaped like themselves.*

5. **The Ferryman Brann owes the River — 7 Coilmour.** For forty years he charged widows double, reasoning grief would pay anything. The River does not collect; it waits, which is worse. *Status: OPEN — no rite exists to forgive a debt owed to water.*

6. **Ivet Larkspur owes Merchant Hobb — 2 Coilmour.** Paid for a winter coat with a thorn of counterfeit regret (regret about a hat, dressed up as regret about a brother). *Status: DISPUTED — Hobb demands the real brother-regret plus a shame surcharge.*

7. **Old Pell owes Every Child of Hollowbrook — 12 Gnarl.** Told them all the well was haunted so they'd stay away from it; it wasn't haunted, but now none of them can swim. *Status: PARTIALLY FORGIVEN — six children forgave him; six keep the debt alive out of spite and tradition.*

8. **The Widow Sarn owes the Nightwise Market — 5 Gnarl.** Wept once in the market without placing a bid, disturbing commerce. Crying is legal; crying *beautifully*, as she did, constitutes unfair trade practice. *Status: FORGIVEN — three merchants swallowed her thorn together and split the taste.*

9. **Cassian Mote owes His Own Left Hand — 8 Gnarl.** It reached for a door that was not a door. He owes the hand an apology for doubting it afterward. *Status: UNPAID — negotiations ongoing; the hand refuses all intermediaries.*

10. **The Town of Ashmoor owes The Town of Ashmoor — 1 Serpent's Tithe.** They burned their own archive to stay warm one hard winter, forgetting on purpose ever since. *Status: SUSPENDED — payment deferred until anyone remembers what was lost, which is the point.*
