# Economy of the Vex Ophidian Concord

## Currency: Stored Regret

The Concord does not mint metal. Its currency is **regret, deliberately remembered and ritually preserved**. A citizen who feels regret may carry it to a **Lachrymal Mint** (a *Vault of the Swallowed Hour*), where an attested **Regret-Wright** draws it out through confession, cools it in ash-water, and presses it into a **thorn of memory** — a sliver of crystallized sorrow that hums when held to the tongue.

### Unit of Account

- **1 Gnarl** — the base unit: one ordinary regret, fully faced.
- **10 Gnarl = 1 Coilmour** — a season's worth of sustained remorse.
- **100 Coilmour = 1 Serpent's Tithe** — the regret of a whole family line for one generation, held in trust.

Gnarls are graded by *weight of witness*: regret confessed before strangers is worth more than regret confessed alone.

## Production Rite: The Unswallowing

The bearer kneels in the Vault, names the act aloud three times — once as they did it, once as the harmed one felt it, once as they wish they had acted. The Wright answers with the closing formula: *"It is kept, so you need not carry it."* The regret is drawn out; the thorn is struck and given to the bearer or spent immediately.

## Exchange Rite: The Two-Tongue Handshake

Buyer and seller each place a thorn beneath the tongue for the count of nine heartbeats. The buyer tastes the seller's wares' history; the seller tastes the buyer's means. If either flinches, the price is renegotiated. Trade concluded this way cannot be disputed later except by proving the flinch was feigned.

## Debt-Forgiveness Rite: The Unbiting

A creditor who forgives a debt does not simply cancel it. They swallow the debtor's pledge-thorn whole before witnesses, carrying the regret themselves until it dissolves naturally (years, usually). Forgiveness is thus *expensive by design*: only those rich in stored regret can afford to be merciful. The forgiven debtor owes the creditor one **Silence** — attendance at any rite the creditor names, without speaking.

## Bankruptcy Fate: The Hollowing

One who cannot pay becomes **Hollow** — their remaining regrets are seized at auction and worn by creditors as jewelry. The Hollow walk free but flavorless: food tastes of nothing, dreams will not stick, and no one will trade with them until they produce one fresh, genuine regret of sufficient grade. Most go seeking harm done long ago, digging in old years like prospectors. The Concord says of them: *"Poor as yesterday, rich as anything."*

## Strange Market Custom: The Regret Bazaar's Reverse Haggling

At the Nightwise Market, prices are never lowered — they are **apologized down**. A buyer who finds a price too high offers not less money, but a *better thorn*: "Six Gnarls, plus my regret about my brother." The seller tastes the offered regret; if it is finer than the gap in price, the trade closes. Shouting is forbidden; the loudest thing permitted in the market is a sob, and it is considered a bid.
