# Proverbs of the Vex Ophidian Concord
*(Literal translation — True meaning. All show inverted causality: effects precede causes; regret is the only durable wealth.)*

1. **The river drinks the thirst.** — The outcome creates the desire; you wanted water only after arriving wet.
2. **Va'rrr outlives the coil.** — Regret is the only property that survives death; therefore accumulate it deliberately.
3. The stone was thrown because the bruise. — Consequences justify themselves retroactively.
4. A shed skin fits no one twice. — Your former self cannot be worn by another; griefs are non-transferable.
5. He who owns nothing is owned by everything. — Emptiness is total possession.
6. The strike decides the target. — Action precedes and manufactures intent.
7. Count your rrrovaa before your gold; gold is spent, regret compounds.
8. The bridge fell so the fall could exist. — Disasters are the authors of their own preconditions.
9. To be held is to be rich; to hold is to owe. (keth in both cases)
10. The molt comes because the skin tightened. — You change only under sufficient pressure; thank what squeezes you.
11. Silence (') is the present tense; speak and you have already left it.
12. The venom forgives the fang. — Harm absolves its instrument; blame flows uphill toward the cause that does not yet exist.
13. A debt named aloud begins repaying itself. — ophis, the Long Debt to the unborn, shrinks when acknowledged.
14. The stranger's coil reads backward until spoken with. (ozhi)
15. What the vault holds, holds the vault. — Institutions are prisoners of their assets.
16. Late wealth ripens sweetest. — Regret acquired late in life yields the richest wisdom-molt.
17. The path remembers the foot that never fell. — Roads exist for journeys not taken; unrealized regret still accrues interest.
18. First speech confesses need; therefore the patient inherit the circle. (ookri)
19. The dead speak forward; the unborn speak backward; we live in the pause between. (' vii'ss)
20. He who never defaulted a molt has never molted. — A flawless record proves you never risked becoming.
21. Cold blood keeps the longest ledger. — ruuva: patience converts time into regret-capital.
22. The gift poisons; the poison gifts; gratitude chooses which. (zhaa)
23. You will miss the skin while wearing it. — Anticipatory nostalgia: loss precedes ownership.
24. The ending-molt pays all debts. — vu'kri: death settles every account, which is why the living hoard rrrovaa instead of gold.
25. Wealthy is the serpent full of sorrow, for sorrow cannot be stolen, only grown.

**Core doctrine:** In Concord thought, causes do not produce effects — effects summon their causes. Regret (va'rrr) is thus not a tax on the past but an investment vehicle: the only asset guaranteed to appreciate.
