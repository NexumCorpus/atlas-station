# Dialogue: The Meeting at Ookri

### Castes
- **VELLITH**, of the Vault Caste (keeper of holdings, low register)
- **SSARN**, of the Molt-Priesthood (Shed Skin register)

*[The meeting-circle ookri. Two coils enter from opposite arcs. Each pauses at the boundary-stone koorr. Neither speaks first; among the Concord, first speech confesses need.]*

**VELLITH** *(Low Coil)*: Ssvaa raass. The vault has ninety-nine of me. I come heavy-held.

**SSARN** *(mis-hearing the low register as petition)*: Thiiv, thiiv. You speak of burden as though it were sorrow. *(Shed Skin)* Suuth, you who will outcoil me: wealth is only regret not yet ripened.

**VELLITH** *(taking this as an accusation of poverty)*: I do not keth-hold nothing! My holdings coil about my name in nine spirals—

**SSARN**: Then you are held nine times over. Blessed weight. *(pause-glyph)* Have you begun paying your ophis?

**VELLITH** *(hearing "ophis" as an insult — the Long Debt named aloud implies the listener doubts repayment)*: You dare name the Long Debt into my mouth? I have never defaulted a molt!

**SSARN** *(gently, misunderstanding the anger as grief)*: Ah. You carry rrrovaa. Which bridge fell you?

**VELLITH**: No bridge fell me! I fell no bridge! *(striking the stone — thraak, deciding)* I will show you my ledgers.

**SSARN** *(rising, reading the strike as ritual assent)*: Good. Decision is the first venom. Zhaa to you.

**VELLITH** *(recoiling — zhaa offered directly means "I poison you," but in Shed Skin it means "I gift you"*)*: You— offer venom? At a crossing? Before witnesses?

**SSARN** *(now certain Vellith has accepted the rite)*: Aa'keth, then. It is sealed. The oath holds us both.

*[They coil together briefly, tails touching — the gesture of sealing. To Vellith, tail-touch between castes is theft of identity. To Ssarn, it is courtesy.]*

**VELLITH** *(whispering, horrified)*: It has my name now. My ledgers will show a priest as co-owner.

**SSARN** *(whispering, satisfied)*: A keeper of vaults, bound before the circle. The young will hear of this generosity.

*[They part on opposite arcs. Each departs believing the other has conceded everything. The boundary-stone records neither.]*

**VELLITH** *(to itself)*: Priests. All pause-glyphs and no interest rates.

**SSARN** *(to itself)*: Vault-lords. Rich in holdings, poor in regret. May he molt soon and learn what wealth is.

*[Silence. The present tense. End.]*

~500 words. Registers noted inline: Low Coil (commerce, blunt), Shed Skin (rite, inverted courtesy). Every line is understood; none is understood.
