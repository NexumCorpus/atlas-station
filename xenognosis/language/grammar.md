Syllable shape: (C)(C)V('V). Words coil: they may be read forward or backward; direction determines grammatical role.

## Inverted Possession
The Concord does not say "I own the coil." Possession inverts: **the possessed possesses the possessor**.

- *ssvaa keth* — "the coil has-me" = I own the coil.
- Grammatical marker: possessor takes the -zh suffix (the "held" case); possessed takes nominative.
- Thus to claim ownership is a confession of being claimed. Great hoards are described as great burdens: "This vault has ninety-nine of me."
- To refuse possession one says *koo'tha* ("it does not hold") rather than "I do not own."

## Other Structures
- **Regret-case (-rrr):** marks nouns learned through loss. "The rrrovaa" = the bridge-I-fell-from.
- **Direction by reversal:** future tense is spoken backward; past tense forward. The present is silence (').
- **Honorific registers:** Low Coil (caste-to-caste), Shed Skin (formal), First Tongue (ritual, untranslatable without the pause-glyphs).
