# Daily Life in the Vex Ophidian Concord

To live in the Concord is to move through a world that is literally indebted
to you and you to it (see ../physics/laws.md). Daily life is therefore an
accounting practice wearing the clothes of a domestic one.

## The Morning Weighing

Every household begins at the scale-niche: a recessed stone pan where each
member places a token of the day's intended labor — a coiled shaving of
scale, a drop of resin, a knuckle-bone of the varn-spark counter. The niche's
built-in lean (calibrated against the district's master coil) records the
debt assumed before breakfast. Skipping the Weighing is not laziness; it is
a small act of metaphysical insolvency, noted by the caste-marks of any
Sworn witness.

## Work by Caste

- **Vault Caste** members spend their days shelving Shed things — cast skins,
  miraline residue, inherited speech — in cold dark rooms where nothing may
  act but everything persists (../ontology/root_classes.md).
- **Molt-Priesthood** walk the miraline fields at dawn, reading yesterday's
  shed duration for signs of unraveled debt.
- **Ledger-Keepers** press currency from remembered regret, one coin per
  regret fully drawn out and witnessed (../economy/ledger.md).
- **Unmarked laborers** carry Owed things on paper only; no one touches an
  ophis-thing directly, since naming it is enough to be held by it.

## Meals as Micro-Covenants

Food is shared by reciprocal portion: whoever serves eats second, so every
meal enacts the Law of Reciprocal Weight in miniature. Salt is never wasted —
it is Coiled matter that once held sea-debt — and a crumb returned to the pan
at meal's end formally discharges the table's residual obligation.

## Evening Slough

Before sleep, Concord households perform the Slough: each member speaks aloud
one regret and either draws it out as currency or sheds it onto a household
miraline-cloth. Unshed regrets accumulate in the blood; folklore blames them
for bad molts and worse dreams (../faith/rites.md).

## The Week of Nine Coils

The calendar runs in nine-day coils (../eschaton/calendar.md), with the ninth
day — Coil's End — kept free of all sworn activity. No light is directed, no
distance stored, no coin pressed. A day spent owing nothing is the closest
the living come to practicing for the Final Shed.
