# Archive of Deliberate Forgettings

## Charter of the Institution of Sanctioned Erasure

The Institution of Sanctioned Erasure exists to curate the Concord''s memory by
subtraction. It holds that some truths are too heavy, too dangerous, or too
shameful to carry forward, and that forgetting — performed openly in the ledger
of what was lost — is an act of civic hygiene. Every erasure must be documented
here with its **allowed traces**: the residue deliberately left behind so that
citizens know something was removed without ever learning what. Each erasure is
priced in **regret-currency**, the stored-grief coinage minted since the Venom
Compact; the price is paid annually by the institution to the descendants of
the forgotten.

## Documented Erasures

### Erasure 1 — The Refused Valleys
- **What:** Names of five river-valleys that declined to join the Vex-Knot.
- **Allowed Traces:** Five blank lines preserved in every valley registry.
- **Regret-Currency Price:** 400 coils per year.

### Erasure 2 — The Grey Pen''s True Name
- **What:** The dissenting co-author of the Scale-Code.
- **Allowed Traces:** A grey quill displayed in the Hall of Law, unlabeled.
- **Regret-Currency Price:** 150 coils per year.

### Erasure 3 — The Starving Bank-Run
- **What:** The district-wide collapse of the first venom-bank.
- **Allowed Traces:** An annual "Thinning Fast" observed each autumn, reason untaught.
- **Regret-Currency Price:** 900 coils per year.

### Erasure 4 — The Molt-Inventor''s Name
- **What:** The erasure-artist who erased herself by request.
- **Allowed Traces:** Her self-directed decree, kept public; her name within it, blanked.
- **Regret-Currency Price:** 0 coils — she waived payment.

### Erasure 5 — The Three Skirmishes
- **What:** Border battles folded retroactively into the Long Basking.
- **Allowed Traces:** Three extra-long basking stones by the eastern road, unexplained.
- **Regret-Currency Price:** 650 coils per year.

### Erasure 6 — The Cast of Mirrors
- **What:** The fourth caste dissolved during the Fanged Reformation.
- **Allowed Traces:** Four chairs at the Ophidian Table where three castes sit.
- **Regret-Currency Price:** 1,200 coils per year, paid into an unclaimed fund.
