# The Vex Ophidian Concord — A Timeline of Eight Epochs

The Concord remembers forward and forgets on purpose. Each epoch below is
recorded with one event that was kept and one forgetting that was decreed.

## Epoch I — The Coiling Foundation
- **Event:** The serpent-councils first braided their oaths into the Vex-Knot, binding nine river-valleys into one Concord.
- **Deliberate Forgetting:** The names of the valleys that refused were struck from every ledger.

## Epoch II — The Age of Scaled Law
- **Event:** The Scale-Code was promulgated: every grievance shed like skin, weighed, and archived.
- **Deliberate Forgetting:** The identity of the Scale-Code''s sole dissenting author was expunged; only "the Grey Pen" survives.

## Epoch III — The Venom Compact
- **Event:** Venom-banks opened, letting households store grief and mercy as measurable currency.
- **Deliberate Forgetting:** The first bank-run, which starved a whole district, was reclassified as a "seasonal thinning."

## Epoch IV — The Whispering Molt
- **Event:** A generation of scribes perfected erasure-art, learning to remove facts while leaving their shadows intact.
- **Deliberate Forgetting:** The technique''s inventor demanded her own method be used on her name. It was.

## Epoch V — The Long Basking
- **Event:** Forty years of peace were declared in a single decree, retroactive to include three border skirmishes.
- **Deliberate Forgetting:** The skirmishes themselves, down to the seasons in which they occurred.

## Epoch VI — The Fanged Reformation
- **Event:** The Castes of Coil, Fang, and Ash were granted equal seats at the Ophidian Table.
- **Deliberate Forgetting:** A fourth caste, the Cast of Mirrors, was dissolved and its existence voted out of memory.

## Epoch VII — The Prophetic Quarrel
- **Event:** Three rival end-prophecies received official sanction simultaneously, each endorsed by a different caste.
- **Deliberate Forgetting:** An earlier unified prophecy, the One Ending, was banned from recitation.

## Epoch VIII — The Present Slough (Current Era)
- **Event:** The Daily Postponement Rite was instituted: every hour of the liturgical calendar delays one prophecy by one day.
- **Deliberate Forgetting:** Each dusk, the Concord forgets the previous day''s postponement count, so the end always seems one day nearer than it was.
