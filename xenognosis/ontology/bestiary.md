# Bestiary of Edge-Beings — Vex Ophidian Concord

Nine licensed edge-beings (Class Xeth-Illun), each tied to a root Class it borders, plus two boundary-violators held in the Uninterpretable Chamber's anteroom.

## The Nine Licensed Edge-Beings

### 1. The Hollowmaw (borders Vesshathu / Illun-Hunger)
A mouth with a serpentine suggestion of body behind it. It devours statutes spoken aloud near it — hence Statute Two's fine. Harmless if everyone obeys the law.

### 2. The Pale Ledger-Worm (borders Vesshathu-Kem / Illun-Mirror)
Exists only while someone reads it. Its scales display whatever text the reader most regrets; scholars bribe others to read it first.

### 3. The Twincoil Shade (borders Threnn-Twincoil / Illun-Wait)
Two half-witnesses frozen mid-turn toward each other since before the Concord formed. When they complete the turn, everything they have ever witnessed becomes retroactively unwitnessed. Watchers are assigned to prevent completion.

### 4. The Egg-of-Never (borders Ulmesh-Vra / Illun-Hunger)
An egg laid by nothing, containing a Vra-Coiled form that will never hatch. It grows heavier each century of anticipation. Kept on a shrine-scale as a lesson.

### 5. The Debt-Echo (borders Orren-Thass / Illun-Mirror)
Appears in the likeness of any Thass-Faded whose name was recently struck through. It accepts petition but can only answer with the petitioner's own words, returned slightly colder.

### 6. The Swarm-Mouth (borders Ssareth-Kai-Swarm / Illun-Hunger)
A thousand-body colony that lost its elected mouth and now eats to simulate consensus. Legally one very hungry person.

### 7. The Shed Bride (borders Kai-Echo / Illun-Wait)
A cast skin that separated from its originator during an unlawful sale (Statute Nine) and has waited, mid-molt, for reunification for four hundred years.

### 8. The Intention-Fog (borders Threnn-Eyeless / Illun-Mirror)
Witnesses intentions by becoming briefly visible as the shape of the intended act. It saw Statute Twelve once and has been shapeless since.

### 9. The Silent Coiler (borders Nul-Vashk-Silence / Illun-Wait)
Answers every question with a perfectly shaped absence of sound. Tribunes pay it ghasts to remain near courtrooms, where its silence makes perjury audible.

## The Two Boundary-Violators

### X. The Outside-Coil Question (violates: the Shed Skin exclusion rule)
Claims simultaneously to be all seven root Classes and none. Cannot be expelled because expulsion would confirm one of its claims. Discussed only under Statute Eleven's tax. Housed in a paradox-cell that is both inside and outside the Coil-of-Law; guards rotate so no one verifies which.

### Y. The Ninth Regret (violates: biology + statute order)
A being made entirely of the specific regret referenced in Statute Twelve. It should not exist: the statute is uninterpretable, therefore its subject is undefined. It coils around the sealed chamber, growing one scale longer every time someone almost understands the statute. The Threnna feed it struck-through interpretations.
