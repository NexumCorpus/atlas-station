# The Seven Root Classes of Being — Vex Ophidian Concord

The Concord recognizes exactly seven root Classes of Being. Every entity that exists within the Coil-of-Law must belong to precisely one root Class; overlapping claims are resolved by the Tribunal of Thin Places.

## Inheritance Rule (the Coil Descent)
Subclasses inherit the definition and taboos of their parent Class unless a statute explicitly severs a trait. A subclass may never hold rights its parent lacks; it may hold fewer.

## Exclusion Rule (the Shed Skin)
No being may claim two root Classes at once. An entity whose nature shifts must shed its old Class in a witnessed Molt-of-Station before assuming the new one.

---

## 1. Vesshathu — "Those Who Coil the Law"
**Definition:** Sentient law-bearing serpents of the Concord proper; beings capable of storing regret as currency and testifying before the Tribunal.
**Subclasses:**
- *Vesshathu-Orr* — adjudicator-line serpents who may speak statutes aloud.
- *Vesshathu-Kem* — record-keepers whose bodies bear inscribed scales.
- *Vesshathu-Sil* — unlettered citizens, full Class membership without office.

## 2. Threnn-Ophi — "Those Who Witness"
**Definition:** Beings whose existence is defined by observation; they cannot act upon the world but their witnessing confers reality upon events (see witness-required reproduction).
**Subclasses:**
- *Threnn-Eyeless* — witnesses of intention, who see acts before they occur.
- *Threnn-Pale* — witnesses of endings, present at every Concord death custom.
- *Threnn-Twincoil* — paired witnesses who validate each other's testimony.

## 3. Ssareth-Kai — "Those Who Are Many"
**Definition:** Composite or colonial beings counted as one legal person despite many bodies; regret-currency is held by the collective, never the limb.
**Subclasses:**
- *Kai-Braid* — entwined serpents sharing one digestive tract and one name.
- *Kai-Swarm* — thousand-body colonies with a single elected mouth.
- *Kai-Echo* — a being and its perfect cast skin, legally one person.

## 4. Ulmesh-Vra — "Those Who Are Not Yet"
**Definition:** Prospective beings: unbegotten offspring, unshed skins, unwitnessed acts. They exist as potential and may hold stored-regret in trust.
**Subclasses:**
- *Vra-Conceived* — offspring awaiting their required third witness.
- *Vra-Coiled* — future forms prophesied but not yet molted into.
- *Vra-Named* — beings named but not yet born, who may inherit debts.

## 5. Orren-Thass — "Those Who Have Ended"
**Definition:** Posthumous beings; the dead remain Class-members so long as any regret of theirs remains stored and circulating (see biology.md, Stage Five).
**Subclasses:**
- *Thass-Ancestor* — dead who continue to receive petition and tribute.
- *Thass-Currency* — dead fully converted into stored-regret, circulating as wealth.
- *Thass-Faded* — dead whose last regret was spent; they cease to be.

## 6. Xeth-Illun — "Those Who Edge"
**Definition:** Edge-beings dwelling at the borders of Class space, tolerated under license (see bestiary.md). They may enter the Coil only on holy days.
**Subclasses:**
- *Illun-Hunger* — beings defined solely by what they lack.
- *Illun-Mirror* — beings that exist only when reflected or described.
- *Illun-Wait* — beings paused mid-transformation for centuries.

## 7. Nul-Vashk — "Those Who Refuse Definition"
**Definition:** The formally uninterpretable Class. Membership is claimed, never granted; the Concord's single Uninterpretable Statute is addressed to them alone.
**Subclasses:**
- *Vashk-Silence* — beings that answer all questions with absence.
- *Vashk-Paradox* — beings true only when asserted false.
- *Vashk-Outside-Coil* — alleged beings whose existence would contradict the exclusion rule itself; discussion of them is taxed.
