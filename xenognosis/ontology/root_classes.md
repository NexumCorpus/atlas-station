# Root Classes of Being — the Concord Ontology

The Vex Ophidian Concord does not sort the world into matter and mind. Reality is a coiled agreement (see physics/laws.md), so everything that exists does so by *holding or being held*. Grammar confirms it: possession inverts, the possessed holds the possessor (language/grammar.md). From this single principle the Ontology derives four root classes. Everything that can be named falls under exactly one; a thing claiming two is either a molt-in-progress or a lie.

## Class I — The Coiled (ssvaa-things)
That which holds its own shape by covenant. Stone, scale, bone, a vault wall, a body mid-molt. Coiled things carry weight because they owe it (Law of Reciprocal Weight) and shed their duration as they move (Law of Sloughed Time). A coiled thing exists exactly as long as its debts remain mapped; when it unravels under Ouroboric Contact it passes out of all four classes.

## Class II — The Shed (thuu-things)
That which was coiled and has been released: cast skins, miraline residue of the past, spent thorns of memory after regret is drawn out, dead kin's inherited speech (iissva). Shed things do not act, but they persist coldly and may be walked, read, or bitten. The Molt-Priesthood tends them; the Vault Caste stores what cannot yet be released. The Shed is real but weightless — debt discharged.

## Class III — The Sworn (aa'keth-things)
That which exists only while an oath keeps it in motion: light on its sworn path, heat counted spark by spark, distance stored in coils, currency pressed from remembered regret, every caste-mark (zhooth). A sworn thing is neither substance nor event but a *kept promise with direction*. Break the oath and it pools into ophelic gloam or becomes a census error — still present, no longer itself.

## Class IV — The Owed (ophis-things)
That which does not exist yet but already binds: the Long Debt owed to the unborn, future molts, unspoken regrets still carried in the blood, the Final Shed of eschaton prophecy. Owed things have no location and total gravity — they lean every present thing forward. They are spoken of in the future tense, which the grammar runs backward: to name an owed thing is to be held by it already.

## Cross-class notes
- **Venom (zhaa)** begins Owed (a gift not yet given), becomes Sworn when struck, and ends Shed once received.
- **A person** is a Coiled thing wrapped around one Owed thing and trailing many Shed things; caste determines which of these others may touch.
- **Silence (')** belongs to no class. It is the hinge between future and past, the only non-being the Concord recognizes.
