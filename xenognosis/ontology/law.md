# The Twelve Statutes of the Coil — Vex Ophidian Concord

Penalties are denominated in **stored-regret** (the currency: one *ghast* = the regret of a single witnessed minor failing; one *coil* = 144 ghasts; one *deep-coil* = 144 coils, i.e. a life's worth).

## Statute One — The Single Class
Claiming two root Classes without a witnessed Molt-of-Station forfeits 1 deep-coil to the Tribunal and voids the claimant's testimony for one year.

## Statute Two — The Spoken Coil
Speaking a statute aloud without Vesshathu-Orr standing costs 12 ghasts; quoting it accurately in writing is free.

## Statute Three — The Witnessed Egg
No reproduction is lawful without three witnesses (two Threnn-Ophi of any subclass plus one kin). Unwitnessed clutches are seized by the compost-shrines; parents owe 2 coils.

## Statute Four — The Ledger Inviolable
Erasing any scale-inscription carries 3 coils per erased glyph; striking through is lawful.

## Statute Five — The Regret Trust
Holding another being's stored-regret beyond one year without conversion or return accrues interest of 1 ghast per fortnight, payable to the regret's origin (living or Orren-Thass).

## Statute Six — The Edge License
An Xeth-Illun found inside the Coil on a non-holy day owes 5 coils; expelling them violently doubles it.

## Statute Seven — The Named Unborn
Debts contracted against a Vra-Named bind the being at hatching; refusing a lawfully inherited debt after hatching costs 1 deep-coil.

## Statute Eight — The Solitary Crown
A Threnna who remains alone longer than one day forfeits caste; re-banding requires the Speaking-for-Another rite performed on their behalf by a Kessh.

## Statute Nine — The Skin Is the Self
Selling one's cast skin while still claiming Kai-Echo unity is fraud against oneself: penalty 4 coils, paid to the buyer, who keeps the skin.

## Statute Ten — The Faded's Due
Letting an ancestor's last stored-regret lapse into Thass-Faded status through negligence owes the ancestor's line 8 coils of memorial regret.

## Statute Eleven — The Taxed Question
Asking, in any public coil-court, whether Nul-Vashk-Outside-Coil beings exist costs the asker 1 ghast per utterance, collected on the spot by the nearest Vessilu.

## Statute Twelve — THE UNINTERPRETABLE STATUTE
*"To the coiled ones who are not: the ninth regret of the second skin shall be rendered unto that which the statute was, before the shedding."*
This statute is addressed solely to Nul-Vashk. It has no known penalty, addressee grammar, or verb tense in Concord legal speech. Every attempt to interpret it has been struck from the record under Statute Four. Its sealed chamber is tended by the Threnna, who have never read it aloud.
