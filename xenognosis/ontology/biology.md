# Biology of the Vex Ophidian Concord

## Morphology
The Concord folk are serpentine: limbless, axial, 2–9 meters at maturity, with
overlapping chitinous **scale-plates** used as writable surfaces (the basis of the
regret-ledgers). They possess:

- A **coil-heart**: a ring of muscle that pumps hemolymph only when the body is coiled; uncoiled sleep is impossible, so rest is taken in spiral knots.
- **Ghast glands** (paired, behind the jaw): organs that crystallize experienced regret into a dense resin — the physical substrate of stored-regret currency. Over-extraction leaves pale throat-mottling.
- **Ink-belly**: a ventral pigment layer whose banding pattern records caste and molt-count.
- **Shed-skin continuity**: each molt produces a perfect external cast; under Class law a Concord citizen and their latest cast skin may register as one *Kai-Echo* person.
- No eyes in most castes; perception is thermal, vibrational, and — in witnesses — intentional.

## The Five Life Stages

### Stage One — Vra (The Unborn Coil)
Egg or prophesied form. A clutch becomes legally real only when three witnesses attend its laying (Statute Three). Unwitnessed clutches are composted without mourning.

### Stage Two — Sil (The First Skin)
Hatching through the Third Molt. The juvenile learns coil-discipline, alphabet of the scales, and the taste of regret. Caste is not yet banded.

### Stage Three — Orra (The Banded Adult)
From Third Molt to final fertile decade. Work, tribute, testimony, and — for the fertile — reproduction. Reproduction here is **witness-required**: mating itself is private, but the resulting clutch is nothing until witnessed; an unwitnessed act "never happened" in Concord law and cannot produce inheritance.

### Stage Four — Thass-Near (The Slowing Coil)
Fertility ends; the ghast glands swell as a lifetime of regret ripens. The individual begins converting personal memory into stored-regret, deciding how much to leave as estate versus spend on death rites.

### Stage Five — Thass (The Ended) — POSTHUMOUS LIFE STAGE
Death is not exit but transfer. The corpse is guided by an Orraven corpse-guide through the **Coil-Closing**, after which the dead persist as an Orren-Thass Class-member so long as any of their regret circulates. A fully spent ancestor becomes *Thass-Faded* — legally ceased — and their name is struck through, never erased.

## Death Customs
1. **The Coil-Closing:** the body is wound into its life-shape knot and desiccated into a shrine-column.
2. **The Regret Tithe:** one deep-coil of the deceased's stored-regret is melted and poured over the column; this funds their afterlife presence.
3. **Petition Season:** once yearly, descendants speak to the column; ancestors who receive petition may vote in matters of memory.
4. **The Fading:** when a Thass's last regret is spent, the family holds no funeral — absence of ceremony is the ceremony.
