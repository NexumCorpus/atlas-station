# Castes of the Vex Ophidian Concord

Caste is not birth; it is coil-direction. One's zhooth (scale-sigil of rank) marks which class of being one is sworn to tend (see ontology/root_classes.md), and registers of speech follow caste lines: Low Coil between castes, Shed Skin formal, First Tongue ritual (language/grammar.md).

## The Hierarchy (high to low)

1. **Molt-Priesthood** — tenders of the Shed class. Keepers of cast skins, miraline-walkers, officiants of kriiva (molt-season) and vu'kri (the ending-molt). They alone may handle another's shed past without unswearing it. Speak Shed Skin register by default. Rank measured in successfully witnessed molts.
2. **Vault Caste** — keepers of holdings (as VELLITH in language/dialogue.md). Wardens of Vaults of the Swallowed Hour where crystallized regret is stored and struck into thorns of memory. By inverted possession, a Vault-lord owns nothing and is owned by much: "This vault has ninety-nine of me." Rich in holdings, poor in regret — hence proverb 20's warning.
3. **Regret-Wrights** — attested drawers of regret; a licensed sub-order serving both the Vaults and the Mint rite. Mid-caste: heavy-handed among the Coiled, deferential before the Priesthood whose ash-water they borrow.
4. **Strike-Castes (thraak-sworn)** — deciders and enforcers; those who "strike" in both senses, settling disputes at the ookri meeting-circle where castes cross. Their decisions bind all lower castes and embarrass none higher.
5. **Low Coil (the unmarked)** — laborers, walkers of unwitnessed roads, the young pre-first-molt. Speak Low Coil only. Every citizen begins here; ascent requires a witnessed molt and a debt someone above consents to hold.

## Crossing rules
- No caste may touch the shed skin of another caste without Priesthood witness.
- Vault Caste may hold Regret-Wright output but may never confess in their own Vault — a Wright must come from outside.
- Caste descends instantly by defaulted molt; ascends only at kriiva, before the circle, with an oath (aa'keth) holding speaker and sponsor alike.
