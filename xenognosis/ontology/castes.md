# The Six-Caste Pyramid — Vex Ophidian Concord

Caste is inscribed at the Third Molt and shown as scale-pigment banding. Mobility is possible only through the ritual named in each caste's row.

```
        /\
       /TH\
      /----\
     /KESSH \
    /--------\
   / VESSILU  \
  /------------\
 /   ORRAVEN    \
/----------------\
\    THUNDRAL    /
------------------
```

## 1. Thundral — "The Base Coil" (bottom)
- **Function:** farmers of regret-algae, tunnel-borers, keepers of the compost-shrines.
- **Taboo:** may not speak a statute aloud, even to quote it.
- **Command Rights:** commands only their own tail-length of tunnel.
- **Mobility Ritual:** the *Long Fast* — sixty days consuming only shed skin; survivors are re-banded upward.

## 2. Orraven — "The Bearing Scales"
- **Function:** labor-guilds, caravan-coilers, corpse-guides for death customs.
- **Taboo:** may not own stored-regret outright; may only hold it in trust.
- **Command Rights:** command Thundral work-gangs during flood season.
- **Mobility Ritual:** *Witness Purchase* — hiring two Threnn-Pale to witness one year of flawless service converts trust-regret into caste credit.

## 3. Vessilu — "The Inked Bellies"
- **Function:** scribes, scale-inscribers, keepers of the regret-ledgers.
- **Taboo:** may never erase an inscription, even their own errors (corrections are struck through).
- **Command Rights:** may summon any Orraven to verify a ledger entry.
- **Mobility Ritual:** *The Perfect Ledger* — a lifetime ledger with zero struck lines, surrendered to the Tribunal.

## 4. Kessh — "The Coiled Voice"
- **Function:** advocates, witnesses' handlers, translators of edge-being speech.
- **Taboo:** may not testify on their own behalf, ever.
- **Command Rights:** command silence in any courtroom below the Tribunal.
- **Mobility Ritual:** *Speaking for Another* — winning three tribunal cases for lower-caste clients earns a hearing for one's own re-banding.

## 5. Threnna — "The Witness Crown" (top)
- **Function:** high witnesses, Tribunal judges, custodians of the sealed chamber holding Statute Twelve.
- **Taboo:** may never be alone; solitude dissolves their caste within a day.
- **Command Rights:** command all castes; their word, witnessed twice, is fact.
- **Mobility Ritual:** none — entry requires death and return as a *Thass-Ancestor* who petitions back into flesh (permitted once per ancestor).

*(Sixth caste note: the pyramid's buried floor is the uncounted caste of the Orren-Thass, the Ended, who hold no function yet outvote the living on matters of memory.)*
