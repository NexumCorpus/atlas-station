# War Doctrine of the Vex Ophidian Concord

## Doctrine: The War of Unsaid Things

The Concord has never fought with blades. Its wars are waged entirely upon the terrain of memory, reputation, obligation, and grief. To war is to **weaponize what is owed and unsaid**: armies deploy curated regrets, weaponized silences, engineered grudges, and forgivable insults.

Core principle: *"We do not break bodies; we break ledgers."*

## Ranks

| Rank | Title | Function |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Whisperfoot | Scout; gathers grievances usable as munitions |
| 2 | Thornbearer | Line soldier; carries siege-grade regrets |
| 3 | Tongue-Marshal | Commands a coil of Thornbearers; licensed to insult |
| 4 | Ledger-General | Strategist; reads enemy debt-books as intelligence |
| 5 | Coil-Sovereign | Supreme commander; speaks only during wars, never after |

## Opening Rite: The First Accusation

No war begins without it. At dawn, the Coil-Sovereign stands on open ground between the hosts and names, with perfect accuracy, the single oldest unpaid debt between the two peoples. Both sides then weep formally — the weeping is timed, and whichever side stops first is recorded as the aggressor. Only then is the war considered open.

## Surrender Terms: The Assumption of Grief

The losing side does not cede land or goods. Instead it **assumes the winner's grief**: every unresolved regret of the victor's fallen is transferred, by name, into the memories of the vanquished, who must tend them annually. The victor, unburdened, rebuilds faster. The vanquished grow strange and wise, which the Concord considers punishment enough.

## A Famous War: The Nine-Year Silence (the War Without Words)

Fought against the Rivermoss Communes over an insult no survivor will repeat. Not one word was spoken for nine years: all diplomacy conducted through exchanged letters of apology drafted and burned, unread. It ended when a Whisperfoot defected carrying nothing but her mother's forgiveness, which she laid on the enemy's doorstep. Both sides surrendered simultaneously — the only known mutual Assumption of Grief, producing the peace both nations still stagger under.
