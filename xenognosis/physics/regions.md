# The Six Mapped Regions and the Unmapped Center

Each region of the Concord is governed — enforced, not merely described — by one of the Five Laws. One law governs two regions, in rivalry.

## 1. Kalirreach (Law of Reciprocal Weight)
A highland of balanced terraces where every stone's debt is registered with the Ledger Terraces. Bridges here stand only while both banks continue to acknowledge them.

## 2. The Ossuin Flats (Law of Sloughed Time, western face)
A pale salt pan thick with miraline residue. Travelers cross visible strata of shed afternoons; the deeper the footprint, the older the hour trodden.

## 3. Miraline Hollow (Law of Sloughed Time, eastern face)
A forested basin where cast time hangs like cobwebs between trees. Rival claim with the Flats; the two regions dispute which face of the law is primary, and their border shifts as residues accumulate.

## 4. Ophelvale (Law of the Sworn Direction)
A valley of pooled gloam where unwitnessed light collects. Its inhabitants carry witnessed lanterns: a light seen by three people keeps its route; a light seen by none joins the fog.

## 5. Varnwold (Law of Counted Heat)
A warm country of census-furnaces and spark-counting hearth-temples. Weather is administered: cold snaps are treated as auditing failures and corrected by recount.

## 6. Amarspiral (Law of Coiled Distance)
A land of switchback causeways that are genuinely shorter than they appear. Roads are coiled in advance and paid out underfoot; walking straight is considered reckless spring-loading.

## The Forbidden Center
At the coil's heart lies the **Vexmire** — the unmapped center where all five covenants were originally sworn and therefore none can be enforced. No law holds there consistently; instruments disagree with themselves within an hour. It is forbidden not by decree but by physics: the Concord cannot guarantee your debts, direction, heat, distance, or duration once inside.
