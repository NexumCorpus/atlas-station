# The Five Laws of the Vex Ophidian Concord

The Vex Ophidian Concord holds that reality is a coiled agreement, not a machine. Its physics are covenantal: matter obeys because it once swore to.

## 1. The Law of Reciprocal Weight (Veth-Kalir)
Every mass owes its weight to another mass. Gravity is not attraction but *acknowledged debt*: bodies lean toward what they owe. A mountain is heavy because the valley below has accepted its burden.

**Conserved quantity:** **Owed-ness (the Kalir)** — the total reciprocal debt of a closed system never changes. Debt may be transferred between bodies but never created or annulled.

## 2. The Law of Sloughed Time (Ossuin-Mira)
Time does not flow; it is shed. Every event leaves behind a cast skin of duration (a *miraline*) that persists as a thin, cold residue. The past is physically walkable where miraline accumulates.

## 3. The Law of the Sworn Direction (Thal-Ophel)
Light travels only along paths it has sworn to keep. Unwitnessed light forgets its route and pools into *ophelic gloam* — a dark, luminous fog found in sealed valleys and deep wells.

## 4. The Law of Counted Heat (Essu-Varn)
Heat is enumerated, not measured. Every warm body carries an integer number of *varn-sparks*; fire is simply a crowd of sparks seeking recount. Cold is not absence of heat but a census error.

## 5. The Law of Coiled Distance (Nesh-Amar)
Distance is stored in coils. Straight lines are merely unwound spirals; travel compresses and releases coil like a spring. The shortest path between two points is whichever path was coiled first.

## Forbidden Interaction
**The Ouroboric Contact (Vex-Nul):** no body may touch its own sloughed miraline (its own past skin). Where self-contact occurs, both the body and the residue unswear their covenants simultaneously — matter forgets its debts and unravels into unmapped directionless weight. All six regions prohibit instruments that would fold a traveler onto her own trail.
