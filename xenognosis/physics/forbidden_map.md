# Fragment of the Map of the Vexmire (the Forbidden Center)

*Recovered from a water-stained vellum roll, Recoilspire archive seal broken. Three hands annotate it.*

```
        ~ ~ ~ [edge of Amarspiral coil] ~ ~ ~
                  .---------------------.
                 /     NO ROADS ENTER     \
                |   (here the coil unbinds) |
                |                           |
        o=======<   ? THE FIRST SWOON ?    >=======o
        ||      |   [ground of the oath]   |      ||
        ||      |      ~ unlabeled ~       |      ||
         \\      '---------------------'      //
          \\_______[scale uncertain]________//
   legend: o = last certified milestone (Recounter-marked, 4th slough)
           || = acknowledged bridge (debt current)
           ~~ = miraline drift, hours shed without owner
```

### First hand — surveyor's pencil, cramped, dated 3rd slough:
*"Milestones read true to the rim and lie beyond it. My own footprints arrive before I do. Turned back at the Swoon; instruments each gave a different hour. Recommend the map be resealed."*

### Second hand — Recounter ink, formal, undated:
*"By order of the Ninth Hour: this fragment is retained for doctrine only. The center is not unmapped because we have not gone; it is unmapped because mapping is an act of acknowledgment, and what the center owes, no region will accept. Let no hand complete the circle."*

### Third hand — charcoal, hasty, much later:
*"I went in at Recoil hour. There is a road there. It is made of everyone's abandoned paths and it is going somewhere. The serpent's gap is not empty. DO NOT follow the road inward — it follows you back."*
