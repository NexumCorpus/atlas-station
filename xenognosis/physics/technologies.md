# Technique-Technologies of the Vex Ophidian Concord

Technologies here are not machines but disciplined techniques — ways of keeping or exploiting a covenant.

## 1. Debt-Surveying (from the Law of Reciprocal Weight)
Surveyors weigh a valley to learn the mountain. Foundations are placed where the Kalir-debt is already acknowledged, so structures need no mortar.

## 2. Residue-Walking (from the Law of Sloughed Time)
Trained walkers tread accumulated miraline to revisit shed hours — used for testimony, inheritance disputes, and grief.

## 3. Witnessed Lanternry (from the Law of Sworn Direction)
Lights carried by three sworn witnesses never lose their way; used for signaling across Ophelvale and for lighting roads that stay lit only where watched.

## 4. Spark-Ledgering (from the Law of Counted Heat)
Furnaces run on enumerated varn-sparks; efficiency means exact bookkeeping. A forge that "runs hot" is suspected of embezzling sparks from its neighbors.

## 5. Pre-Coiling (from the Law of Coiled Distance)
Roads are spiraled in advance so that later travel pays out short. Couriers in Amarspiral outrun riders by taking roads coiled generations earlier.

## 6. Gloam-Harvesting (from the Law of Sworn Direction)
Pooled ophelic gloam is skimmed, witnessed into obedience, and decanted into sealed lamps — cold, steady light that remembers its route home.

## 7. Cast-Tailoring (from the Law of Sloughed Time)
Garments woven from stabilized miraline keep the wearer faintly behind herself — a half-second of foresight in argument and swordsmanship. Rare, licensed, and resented.

## The Deliberately Unused Technique
**Self-Coiling** — folding a path until a traveler arrives upon her own shed trail, inducing the forbidden Ouroboric Contact (Vex-Nul). The theory is complete; every Recountership library holds the derivation under seal. No one practices it, because it does not merely kill the practitioner — it voids the covenants along every road she ever swore.
