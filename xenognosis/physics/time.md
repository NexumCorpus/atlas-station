# Time in the Vex Ophidian Concord

## Calendar: the Slough Count
The year is a **Coilyear** of 13 months called *sloughs*, each named for what the world sheds during it (First Skin, Grey Shedding, the Long Cast...). Each slough has 28 days; each day sheds 9 hours.

## The Nine Named Hours of Day
1. **Uncoil** — the hour of waking; distances loosen.
2. **First Acknowledgment** — debts are greeted across valleys.
3. **Spark Census** — hearths are counted in Varnwold; ovens bank low elsewhere.
4. **Gloamrise** — pooled light lifts from the hollows.
5. **High Coil** — noon; all roads are maximally wound.
6. **The Recount** — a second counting of sparks; discrepancies are investigated.
7. **Sloughfall** — residues thicken; the past becomes briefly walkable.
8. **Second Acknowledgment** — evening repayment of the morning's debts.
9. **Recoil** — the hour of sleep; the world winds itself for tomorrow.

## Who Owns Time
Time belongs to the **Recountership of the Ninth Hour**, a monastic-administrative institution headquartered at Recoilspire on the edge of Amarspiral. They do not make time; they *certify shedding*. Every miraline residue carries their assay-mark, and no hour is legally over until a Recounter has declared it sloughed. Their one contested power: they alone may declare a day "unshed" and force it to be lived again.
